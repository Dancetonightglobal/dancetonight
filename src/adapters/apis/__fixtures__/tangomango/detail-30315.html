Miami Tango Role Switch Championship & Festival
November 6–8, 2026 Miami, Florida**

The world's first Argentine Tango Championship & Festival dedicated to * Role Switching* —the exploration, understanding, and mastery of both dance roles: **Leader and Follower**.

Join dancers from around the world for an unforgettable weekend of workshops, championship, milongas, performances, live music, and embraces exchange.
