NYC’s Thursday Home for Lindy Hop!

NYC's go-to spot for Lindy Hop, featuring FREE intro lessons and dancing to today's hottest DJs and favorite live bands. Home for the NYC swing dance community since 2001!

When:

Every Thursday (Exceptions listed in our Full Calendar [https://frimframjam.com/calendar] )

8:30-9pm FREE intro lesson

9pm-Close Open dancing to NYC’s favorite DJs and live bands

Admission:

$15 ($10 w/ student ID) for DJ nights [https://frimframjam.com/calendar?category=DJ]

$25 ($20 w/ student ID) for live music on Third Thursdays [https://frimframjam.com/calendar?category=Third%20Thursdays]

Credit/cash accepted at the door

Admission & pre-sales for special events as listed

Where:

37 W 26th St, 2nd floor, NYC [https://goo.gl/maps/3co1To3LjisNgta77]

Beautiful venue! Gorgeous sprung dance floor!

Intense A/C! Huge fans! Epic ventilation!

Held at the same great studio - You Should Be Dancing…! [https://www.youshouldbedancing.nyc/]

How:

😷 Masks remain optional for all of our events, and we continue to have free high-quality masks available to anyone who wants one

🔑 Check event details [https://frimframjam.com/calendar] for monthly door code

Frim Fram Jam is a proud and grateful recipient of the Sw!ng Out Community Fund [https://www.swingout.show/community-fund] grant program.

Frim Fram Jam and its staff consider it a safe place for all attendees. Please read our Code of Conduct [https://frimframjam.com/code-of-conduct] and ensure we foster a welcoming community.
