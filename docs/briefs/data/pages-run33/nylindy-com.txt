New York Lindy Exchange (NYLX)
October 9-11, 2026
New York City, NY

New York Lindy Exchange 2026 (NYLX) promises an exceptional lineup of engaging workshops and dance experiences. This year’s theme is: Lindy Hop – The Past is Prologue!

It's time to not only celebrate the rich history of Lindy Hop, but also to explore its evolution into various dance styles. This event will delve into the African roots of Lindy Hop while showcasing how these foundational elements have contributed to the development of other dances and movements.

Participants can look forward to sessions led by some of the finest instructors in NYC that will enhance your Lindy Hop skills and musicality.

This event holds special significance, and we warmly invite you to join us. We are excited to present lower package prices, more flexible options, and greater value for your money!
